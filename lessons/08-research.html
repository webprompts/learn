Module 3: Real Uses

Research & Learning

2 min read
AI is the world's most patient tutor. It can explain anything at your level, answer endless follow-ups, and never judge your questions.

Whether you're learning a new skill, researching a topic, or trying to understand something complex, AI can accelerate your learning dramatically.

Learning Prompts

The Explain Like I'm 5

Simple explanations
Explain [concept] to me like I'm a complete beginner. - Use simple language, no jargon - Use a concrete analogy from everyday life - Then, once I understand the basics, give me the more technical version

The Curriculum Builder

Learn anything systematically
I want to learn [skill/topic]. My current level: [beginner/intermediate/etc.] Time I can dedicate: [hours per week] My goal: [what I want to be able to do] Create a learning roadmap with: 1. Key concepts to master (in order) 2. Best free resources for each 3. Practical exercises to build real skills 4. How I'll know when I'm ready to move on

The Deep Dive

Research a topic
I'm researching [topic] and need a comprehensive overview. Please cover: - What it is and why it matters - Key concepts and terminology - Different perspectives or schools of thought - Common misconceptions - Where experts disagree - Best sources to learn more Format as a structured briefing I can reference later.

The Follow-Up Machine

The real power is in follow-up questions. Unlike Google, AI remembers context:

Keep going deeper
"Wait, explain [specific part] more" "How does that relate to [other thing]?" "Give me an example of that" "What would happen if [scenario]?" "Why is it done that way instead of [alternative]?"
Important caveat

AI can make confident mistakes, especially with recent events, statistics, or niche topics. For anything important, verify key facts from reliable sources. AI is great for understanding concepts and getting oriented—less reliable for precise data.

Try It Now

Think of something you've been curious about or wanted to learn. Use the "Explain Like I'm 5" prompt to get a clear explanation, then ask 2-3 follow-up questions to go deeper.

Key Takeaway
AI is a patient, personalized tutor. Ask follow-ups, request analogies, and verify important facts.
