LESSON 1 Foundation

Stop Repeating Yourself.
Train Your AI Once.

Build custom Skills that work exactly how you want — every single time.

The Problem
😤

"Like last time..."

Explaining your preferred format, tone, and style. Every. Single. Chat.

🔄

Copy-paste prompts

A notes doc somewhere with prompts you reuse. Clunky and error-prone.

😩

Inconsistent results

Sometimes AI nails it. Sometimes it's way off. Never predictable.

Build it once. Use it forever.

Skills are reusable AI workflows. Define how you want something done, save it, and call it by name anytime.

How To Do It

Build Your First Skill

1

Enable Skills

Go to Claude settings, find Capabilities, turn on Skills. Also enable "skill-creator" — that's what builds your custom workflows.

Settings → Capabilities → Skills → On
2

Describe What You Need

Start a fresh chat. Tell Claude you want to create a Skill. Be specific about format, tone, structure — everything.

💡

Pro tip: Have an old project that turned out great? Share it as an example.

3

Answer Questions

Claude asks clarifying questions to nail down details. This ensures your Skill works perfectly every time.

4

Download & Install

Claude generates your Skill file. Download it, go to Settings → Skills, upload, toggle on.

Download → Settings → Skills → Upload
5

Use It

New chat, call your Skill by name. It runs exactly how you designed it. Every time.

🔄

Need tweaks? Go back to original chat, tell Claude what to fix, upload new version.
